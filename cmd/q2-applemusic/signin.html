Apple Music on Q2

Sign the Q2 in to your Apple Music account.

Enter the PIN on the Q2's screen

On the Q2, open Streaming → Apple Music and pick what to download. You can close this page.

1. Copy your media-user-token from a computer

It's the sign-in cookie music.apple.com keeps. The Q2 has no browser, so it borrows yours.

  1. On a computer, sign in at music.apple.com and play any song once.
  2. Open the developer tools: F12, or ⌥⌘I on a Mac.
  3. Chrome / Edge: Application → Cookies → https://music.apple.com.
    Firefox: Storage → Cookies → https://music.apple.com.
    Safari: Storage → Cookies (turn on Develop in Settings → Advanced first).
  4. Copy the Value of the cookie named media-user-token: a long run of letters and digits.

A cookies.txt exported from music.apple.com works too: paste all of it.

2. Paste it here

It's checked with Apple, then saved on the Q2's card in .applemusic/media-user-token. Treat it like a password: anyone with it can use your account. An Apple Music subscription is needed: songs download to the Q2's card as AAC 256 kbps, then play from it.
